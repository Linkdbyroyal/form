# Product Vision (summary, max 200 words)

Second Brain Business Transformation OS is the core product of Second Brain AI Agency: a repeatable method plus a working multi-tenant system that investigates a company, improves its processes, automates what is approved and then measures the result. A small pool of specialised agents, coordinated by an orchestrator, works on four levels: strategy, departments, processes, execution and monitoring. Agents ask only the questions that close an evidence gap, label every statement as client claim, data observation or hypothesis, and never present an estimate as a fact.

Each client gets a sealed workspace with its own dashboards, Knowledge Vault, approvals and KPIs. Our team runs everything from Mission Control. Our own methods, skills and templates live in the Agency Intelligence Vault and flow to clients only as versioned, reviewed templates; nothing flows back without review and rights checks.

What it is: a consultancy delivery platform with auditable AI agents, human approval gates and measurable outcomes.

What it is not: a chatbot, a generic BI tool, an autonomous company, a system that moves money, hires people or signs contracts, or a model that trains on client data.
